# Around the Table: Questions

The question bank for Game 3 (rules.md). Each round shows one question about the subject,
with four options. The subject picks their real answer; everyone else predicts it.

Edit freely: change wording, swap options, add or remove questions. The code copies this
list. Rules for a good question:

- Exactly four options, A to D, short enough to fit on a phone button (about 40
  characters).
- All four should be believable for someone at the table, so it's a real guess.
- Light and fun. Nothing someone would regret having on the big screen.

There are 35 questions; a match uses one per player (up to 10), plus any skipped, never repeating within a
room until all have been used.

## 1. Everyday Personality

1. **What's your ideal way to spend a completely free afternoon?**
   A. Out doing something active · B. On the couch with a show or game ·
   C. Hanging out with friends · D. Working on a hobby or project
2. **What's a small purchase that instantly improves your day?**
   A. A good coffee or drink · B. A snack or treat · C. Something for a hobby ·
   D. A new game, book, or song
3. **What role do you naturally take when planning a group trip?**
   A. The planner with the spreadsheet · B. The one who finds the food spots ·
   C. "I'm down for whatever" · D. The one who forgets to pack something
4. **Routine or variety?**
   A. Same routine every day · B. Mostly routine, small surprises ·
   C. Mostly new things, a few habits · D. Something different every single day
5. **What's your preferred way to spend a Friday night?**
   A. Going out · B. Staying in and recharging · C. A small hangout at someone's place ·
   D. Gaming or a movie marathon
6. **What's one thing you always make time for, no matter how busy?**
   A. Working out · B. A real meal · C. Talking to friends or family ·
   D. Gaming, shows, or a hobby
7. **What would you rather have more of?**
   A. Free time · B. Travel · C. Money · D. Sleep
8. **What do you procrastinate on the most?**
   A. Chores · B. Replying to messages · C. Appointments and errands ·
   D. Work or school deadlines
